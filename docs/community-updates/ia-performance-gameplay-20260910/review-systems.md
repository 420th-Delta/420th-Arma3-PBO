## Integration defects handled during the merge

The incoming radio function assigns its third argument to the old body; upstream assigns that argument to the exact target body. A textual overwrite would break upstream's delayed-killed-event protection. Resolution retains upstream's third argument and puts the old body fourth. The incoming access cache also lacked upstream donor entitlement; donor expiry and renewal are now cache inputs, preserving revocation. The five owned conflict resolutions retain upstream spawn and cleanup telemetry, including a closed measurement on rejected group admission.

## Review observations and acceptance gaps

- The vehicle-handler fix is small and coherent: it stops clearing an already-restored HandleDamage ID during locality handoff, allowing the existing validity check to prevent duplication. Dedicated server/client ownership transfer remains the required engine test.
- Laser labels use a 250 ms cache invalidated by observer, vehicle, or side; live target positions still refresh each Draw3D event. The new default for missing labels avoids indexing absent ownership data. This is a bounded display-latency tradeoff; no introduced defect found in static review.
- `serverDetector` moves the engine area filter before SQF side filtering, preserving result order and the special EAST count mode. `hcCore` schedules its next scan from completion. Both changes are independently reviewable; neither establishes a measured FPS gain.
- BLUFOR speech uses the existing creation/JIP/respawn hook and adds a Local handler for owner-only RADIOPROTOCOL settings. [Bohemia's disableAI reference](https://community.bistudio.com/wiki/disableAI) documents RADIOPROTOCOL suppressing reports while retaining orders. Test AI-to-player takeover, respawn namespace copying, joining a different side after creation, and server/headless ownership changes. The code does not install a general group-side-change observer; this is an acceptance gap rather than an established ordinary-spawn failure.
- The new public Side channel is absent from the unchanged staff channel-management menu, which enumerates channels 2, 3, 7 and 13. Staff can still manage those channels, but have no equivalent GUI switch for the new Side voice channel. Decide whether this is intended community policy before accepting the radio feature.
- The ground helper is global behavior, including unmodified consumers. It can reject a formerly valid spawn when all 81 cells fail or cannot fit the requested squad with 15 m separation. `aoEnemy`, `scEnemy`, `taskPatrol`, `taskAttack`, and FOB gained some null/empty guards, but not every caller is updated. Test mandatory objective guards, side missions, sector reinforcement, and existing-group calls, and report rejected-spawn rates in the engine. The per-machine claims are not a distributed reservation across simultaneous server/headless spawns.
- The ground helper executes the entire search under `isNil`: spatial scans, up to 81 clearance searches, water tests, optional visibility rays, and greedy selection complete without scheduled yielding. This preserves a local reservation transaction but can concentrate expensive engine work into a frame. No engine timings were supplied to substantiate a performance improvement. Test slow/blocked urban terrain and repeated rejected admissions under load.
- Housekeeping changes gameplay as well as performance: arsenal holders expire after their queued deadline even near players; previously persistent deployed logistics can be dismantled after five observed unattended minutes; existing ruins are hidden and observed dynamic ruins can be deleted. The holder timestamp belongs to the container, not every subsequent inventory addition. Test multiple drops into one holder, attachments/cargo/ropes, deployed linked equipment, landed versus hovering aircraft, and a ground player returning during the final check.
- Kavala's bounded evacuation frees the mission slot while object and terrain cleanup remain in the shared collector. Test restarting Kavala before its old collector work finishes. Old `UNHIDE_DISCREET` work has no activity identifier, while initialization still toggles the same terrain objects. This lifecycle overlap needs engine acceptance; the prior whole-area waiter also required scrutiny, so it is not classified here as a new proven regression.
- The contributor's offline claims do not include runnable test fixtures in this package. Their stated assertions are not independent evidence of engine behavior, multiplayer locality, or FPS.

## Commit boundaries

The following six independent commits are supportable: laser-label cache; detector filter order; headless scan rescheduling; spawn-menu vehicle handler correction; BLUFOR speech suppression (two TGC files); radio policy and lifecycle (seven radio-related files, including relevant respawn/config portions). Keep the radio merge adaptations in that radio commit.

The remaining gameplay work crosses helper functions, registrations/security, AI controllers, player support, shared core state, and cleanup. Keeping the remaining 46 files together is safer than producing intermediate commits with missing runtime functions or mismatched lifecycle contracts. If smaller commits are required later, split by semantic hunks with compilation and runtime smoke checks at each boundary: shared ground admission plus every dependent caller; housekeeping plus Put/BuildingChanged; Kavala lifecycle plus revive policy/core; then AI/support/Mega Defense state integrations. File-only partitioning of `fn_core`, `fn_AI`, `fn_aoDefend`, and registrations is insufficient.

No recommendation is made here to extract `fn_AIXSuppressiveFire` without its AI review: that file's caller/context compatibility belongs to the delegated AI review.
